ECONOMICS PROBLEM SPACE

Turn today’s economic news into problems people can work on.

Atlas Harbor saves recent publication headlines first, then enriches them in small AI batches with decision framing, questions, topics, and optional current-source context. A slow or incompatible model no longer blocks the news feed.

How this feed works

The original publication title, date, and link remain the canonical source. Headline ingestion is independent from AI enrichment. The administrator’s saved OpenRouter model is used for decision framing; a saved Perplexity key can add current context and citations. Sign in to write your own analysis, publish a shareable article, and optionally attach the underlying economic decision brief beneath your post.
