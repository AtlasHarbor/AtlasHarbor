Atlas Harbor

LIFE SCIENCES PROBLEM SPACE

Turn a biological question into an inspectable research system.

Structure hypotheses, evidence, experimental dependencies, failure modes, translational paths, and uncertainty without confusing an AI-generated proposal with validated science.

Research question

Define the mechanism, population, intervention, comparator, measurable outcome, and time horizon.

Evidence map

Separate primary data, replicated findings, preprints, reviews, assumptions, and unresolved contradictions.

Experimental plan

Track controls, sample size assumptions, assays, materials, sequencing, bottlenecks, safety constraints, and stop criteria.

Translation path

Map what must happen between an interesting result and a useful diagnostic, therapy, platform, or operational change.

Initial scope

This first version establishes the problem model and publishing destination. Future iterations can add protocol templates, dataset connectors, literature ingestion, reproducibility checklists, and structured AI critique. It is not a clinical decision tool and must not be used to replace qualified scientific, medical, biosafety, or regulatory review.
