How Atlas Harbor chooses

Intent

Natural-language needs are treated as a fit problem, not a rigid category match.

Availability

Closed places are excluded when Open now is selected, and closing soon is penalized.

Confidence

Ratings are adjusted for review count so a tiny sample does not dominate.

Tradeoffs

Distance, price, service format, group fit, and review evidence all affect rank.

Restaurant facts may change quickly. Verify hours, reservations, accessibility, and dietary requirements directly with the restaurant. Google Places is the canonical external source when configured.
