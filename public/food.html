FOOD DISCOVERY

Find the best place to eat for this moment.

Search by your current location or any city. Compare cuisine, distance, opening information, ratings, review themes, and your own priorities. The goal is not a universal ranking—it is the best fit for your group, time, mood, budget, and constraints.

The decision model

Context

Where are you, how much time do you have, and who is eating?

Fit

Cuisine, dietary needs, atmosphere, price, availability, and travel effort.

Evidence

Structured place data, provider reviews when configured, and Atlas Harbor community notes.

Choice

A qualitative recommendation with uncertainty and explicit tradeoffs.

Restaurant facts may be incomplete or change quickly. Verify hours, reservations, and dietary requirements directly with the restaurant.
