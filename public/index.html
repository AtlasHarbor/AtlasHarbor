WHY LOGISTICS?

Logistics makes invisible strategy visible.

Most difficult decisions are networks in disguise. They contain scarce resources, dependencies, queues, timing constraints, alternate routes, and failure points. Logistics gives those forces a concrete form you can operate instead of merely discuss.
