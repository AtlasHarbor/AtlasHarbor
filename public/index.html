THE CORE LOOP

Win work, open lanes, move cargo, outmaneuver rivals.

01📄

Accept a contract

A contract names the cargo, origin, destination, capacity needed, deadline, revenue, and failure penalty.

02🗺️

Develop a trade lane

Connect the exact locations required by the contract and decide where recurring service is worth funding.

03🚢

Put ships and capacity on the lane, run the shipment, or sell assets when the market changes.

04⚔️

Rival lanes alter prices and access, revealing how competition changes strategy in other domains.
